# Fractal portfolio — Phase 0–2 design specification

## Entrance

- Initial viewport: oblique view into five nested metallic mirror frames over a black reflective floor.
- Materials: polished silver, graphite, low-opacity steel mirror, restrained white/blue illumination.
- Typography: large editorial headline in the negative space at left; technical depth label and travel cue.
- Arrival: nested frames are separated and the camera approaches from the upper-right.
- Exploration: the mirror installation remains readable while the camera inspects the recursive depth.
- Departure: frames converge, the mirror surface opens, and the camera crosses into the porcelain identity field.

## Identity

- Environment: a bright porcelain plane with two intersecting sculptural forms—one ordered and one expressive.
- Materials: ivory matte, black geometric mass, translucent dark surface, soft warm and cool point lights.
- Typography: content sits in the negative space created by the two forms rather than in a standard card layout.
- Arrival: dark entrance floor gives way to the lighter identity plane.
- Exploration: the forms settle while the About content becomes readable.
- Departure: the identity opening is reserved for the next phase's experience dimension.

The five remaining dimensions are intentionally not implemented in this phase.
